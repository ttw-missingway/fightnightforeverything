# Situation catalogue — 120 candidates

**Edit this file directly.** Strike what doesn't feel like your game, add what I
missed, reword anything whose framing is off. This becomes the spine of the
corpus, so the shape of this list is the shape of the dialogue.

Every trigger is something the sim already tracks — I checked. A situation the
engine can't detect is a scene that never plays.

Notation: `[2]` = two-hander, `[3]` = three people, `[1]` = someone talking to
the room. `†` = needs `arcade:established` (assumes real elapsed time).

## Notes for you

- **Roughly 80% two-handers** on purpose — three-person scenes need three people
  who all satisfy conditions, so they play far less often.
- **The `†` ones need real elapsed time** and will be gated so they cannot fire
  in a young arcade.
- **What I could not derive from the sim:** anything about your friends
  specifically, running gags, or house culture. If there are things that
  actually happen in your scene, those are the ones worth adding by hand — they
  are the situations nobody could generate.
